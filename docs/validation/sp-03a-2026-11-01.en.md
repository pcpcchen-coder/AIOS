## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Collection

- “Record my voice”: 10 wake words (Hey Nous and Hello Nous in turn) plus 10 everyday sentences different from enrollment, each after a chime; a wake word needs at least 400 ms of voice and a sentence at least 2 s.
- “Play stranger voices”: the system's Chinese and English speech voices take turns speaking wake words and sentences, 30 per segment kind (collection) or 50 (verification); a window with no voice picked up does not count.
- Each feature goes to the core as `calib_trial`. The core scores it against the mean direction of each member's **templates of the same kind** (as real recognition does), stores the scores and zeroes the feature at once; the reply is only the summary report, never this trial's scores.
- Trials before thresholds are applied are `collect`; after, `verify`.
- A trial records each member's voice template version at that moment; after a re-recording, deletion or new member, trials with different versions are no longer used. If the speaker model changes, the whole calibration file no longer applies.

### 3.2 Proposed thresholds

- Each segment kind needs at least 10 member samples and 30 stranger trials.
- Impostor scores: a stranger's highest score against any member; a member sample's highest score against other members (with two or more members).
- Proposed `match` = highest impostor score + 0.05, at least 0.3 and at most 0.95; `margin` stays at the starting 0.08.
- The report also lists the proposed threshold's reject rate on the collected data (the share of member samples not recognised as themselves). **Thresholds only move in the safe direction:** they are never lowered to reduce rejects (17 §4.4).

### 3.3 Apply and verify

- “Apply proposed thresholds” first shows a native dialog from the main process with each segment's threshold and reject rate; segments with too little data keep the starting value.
- After applying, matching at wake, during calls and in “Test voice” all use the new thresholds.
- Verification: trials after applying are judged with the current thresholds. A stranger taken for any member, or a member sample taken for another member, counts as a false accept.
- A segment kind is calibrated (`calibrated`) only with ≥50 verification stranger trials and 0 false accepts. One false accept leaves it uncalibrated; collecting again means clearing the calibration data and starting over.
- “Clear calibration data” also needs a native confirm: it deletes the scores and the applied thresholds and returns to the starting values; family voice enrollments are not affected.

### 3.4 Naming after calibration

- Used only for calls started by the wake word, when the face is not recognised (the same conditions as SP-02a's test switch).
- The segment that recognised the person (the wake word or pre-connection speech) must be a calibrated segment; otherwise no name unless the test switch is on. 17 §4.4: a name is allowed only after the matching segment type is calibrated.
- The fusion result is `voice_only_calibrated`, and the greeting decision log says 「聲音門檻已校準」 (voice thresholds calibrated). A name given by voice is still not offered to the memory layer as a face candidate.
- No name while the face disagrees (a stranger's face, several faces, another member recognised earlier in this tracking generation).

### 3.5 Privacy

- Calibration data is scores only, sealed in the family recognition folder; no recordings or features.
- The page sees only the summary report (counts, the lowest and median member score, the highest stranger score, proposed and current thresholds, verification counts), shown only in the manage screen the user opened.
- Logs record only “applied (which segment uses calibrated values)”, “cleared” and `calib_*` op success; never names, `person_id` or scores.
- Stranger voices are local system speech voices; no network.

## 7. Risks

- System speech voices are very different from real voices, so stranger scores may come out low and the proposed threshold too loose; real strangers or people on TV may still be taken for a family member. Calibrated only means “0/100 on this set of synthetic voices”, not a general error rate.
- Playback through the speakers and back through the microphone depends on the room, volume and the Mac's echo cancellation; scores may differ in a new setting, and then calibration should be cleared and redone.
- With only one member, `margin` has no effect and confusion between members cannot be measured.
