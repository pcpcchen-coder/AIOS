Date: 2026-11-01, Asia/Taipei. Branch: `claude/sp-02a-wake-voice` (based on main `66c5348`). Scope: the first half of SP-02 in [17 §13.4](../17_Wake_Word_and_Speaker_Recognition_Plan_2026-10.en.md#134-交付批次與估時). Rules: [ADR-0003 draft](../adr/ADR-0003-local-wake-and-voice-recognition.en.md). Voice enrollment: [SP-00 / SP-01 validation](sp-00-2026-11-01.en.md).

**Thresholds are uncalibrated.** The voice matching thresholds are starting values; only George has tried this on the Mac, and there are no recognition-rate numbers. Naming by voice alone is a **test switch** George asked for, off by default; enabling it for real waits for SP-03 calibration.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Voice matching

- Each segment is compared by cosine with the mean direction of each family member's **templates of the same kind**: the wake-word span only against the 5 enrolled wake words (text-dependent, 17 §4.4), pre-connection speech only against the 3 read sentences.
- Matched requires the top score ≥ `match` and a lead over second place ≥ `margin`: wake word 0.5 / 0.08, speech 0.55 / 0.08. Too little voice (wake word 300 ms, speech 1500 ms) is `too_short` and is not compared. **These numbers are starting points, uncalibrated.**
- Two segments pointing at different people make the whole wake a conflict.

### 3.2 Face-voice fusion (17 §7.3)

Used only for calls started by the wake word; look-to-talk follows ID-02 and ignores voice.

| Face | Voice | Result | Name |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recognises P | Recognises P | `confirmed` | Yes |
| Recognises P | Recognises Q, or two segments point at different people | `conflict` (reason `voice_conflict`) | No |
| Recognises P | Unknown / ambiguous, and P has a voice enrolled | `voice_unsupported` | No |
| Recognises P | No usable voice, or P has no voice enrolled | `face_only` | ID-02 rules |
| No face result | Recognises P | `voice_only` | No (see §3.3 when the test switch is on) |
| Stranger's face, several faces, can't tell which member | Any | — | No |

When P has no voice enrolled, an unrecognised voice does not count against P: there were no templates to compare with. This is this batch's reading of §7.3 “matched P + unknown”, so that face naming does not get worse for members who have not recorded a voice yet.

### 3.3 Test switch “Name by voice alone (test)”

- Voice is used only for calls started by the wake word and only when the face is **not recognised**: page-side conditions fail (not looking at the screen, out of near range), the camera is off or recognition is unavailable, there is no evidence, the tracking generation changed, or evidence is insufficient.
- No name while the face disagrees: a stranger's face (unknown), can't tell which member, several faces, or another family member recognised earlier in this tracking generation.
- No name when naming is off or the cloud naming notice has not been seen.
- A name given by voice is not offered to the memory layer as a face person candidate.
- It can only be turned on from the menu bar, after the main process shows a native explanation: thresholds uncalibrated, the wrong person may be named, a recording or a similar voice may be taken for a family member, the name is sent to the cloud voice provider. A page POST of `voice_name_test: true` always returns 403; the page can only turn it off.

### 3.4 Privacy and boundaries

- Wake-word span: copied once, at detection, and sent straight to the speaker worker over a worker-to-worker MessagePort; overwritten with zeros once the feature is computed. Without a speaker worker nothing is copied.
- Pre-connection speech: stays in local memory only, is finished before the greeting decision and turned into a feature; never sent to the cloud (17 §14 ⑦).
- Only the 192-d feature, voiced milliseconds, segment kind and `wake_id` go to the core. `voice_probe` replies only ok, so the page never learns who was recognised; refusals give no reason.
- “Test voice” is only in the manage screen the user opened; it returns who was recognised, without scores.
- Logs record only segment states and the fusion result, e.g. “聲音 喚醒詞 matched／話語 too_short，融合 voice_only_test”; never names, `person_id` or scores.
- Static checks: `tests/audio-privacy.test.ts` lists every `postMessage` in `src/audio` and checks the wake-word span is copied once, at detection; `tests/speaker-enroll.test.ts` checks the `src/speaker/` file list, no network, storage, IPC or console, and that worker messages carry no samples.
- With `NOUS_WAKE` off, `/api/settings` and the menu are byte-identical to before (`tests/wake-flag-off.test.ts` passes).

## 7. Risks

- Uncalibrated thresholds: wrong or missing recognition is possible. With the test switch on, a similar-sounding family member, a recording or the TV may be called by a family member's name. This only affects how people are addressed; it authorises nothing.
- The speaker worker stays loaded while the wake controller exists (about 28 MB of model plus wasm); idle, it computes nothing and uses no CPU; memory was not measured.
- Starting a call leases an extra microphone copy for pre-connection speech, at most until the greeting decision; the effect on how quickly wake listening resumes was not measured.
